Another way in: tap an NFC tag

At the stall, a compatible NFC tag can open the same website with a phone tap. Write https://mindmesh-netra.web.app/ as a URL to your NFC tag, then place it beside the QR. A physical tag must be prepared first; this page does not program a tag.
