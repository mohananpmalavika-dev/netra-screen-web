ABOUT THIS PROJECT

YIP research and a little rest for your eyes.

NETRA-SCREEN has two parts: the main eye-movement research tool and camera-free rest exercises.

The research tool

Follow four target paths while your phone processes eye landmarks. The report describes experimental observations. It cannot confirm normal vision, diagnose an eye condition or prescribe glasses.

The camera starts only when you choose Start. Processing happens on this device. Camera images are not stored or uploaded. Pausing or leaving the page stops the camera. Children need guardian supervision.

Rest exercises

Look into the distance, blink gently or close your eyes for a short rest. The timer guides you. These exercises use no camera and create no test results. Timers pause when you switch away from the page; choose Resume to continue. They do not replace glasses or treatment.

Your data and offline access

The rest page does not save records. Research reports stay in the current session unless you download or print them. Copies saved outside the app must be deleted separately.

Older saved activity summaries, if present on this browser, are not displayed in this version. The app does not erase them automatically. To remove those records and offline files, clear this site's data in browser settings.

Keep the page online until the footer says offline activities are ready. The project overview and rest exercises then work offline. The original presentation PDF is a separate download.

Stop an exercise if it causes discomfort. For persistent eye discomfort or changes in vision, see an eye doctor.
