DEMONSTRATION SEQUENCE

The 90-Second Walkthrough Stages

STAGE 01 · 00-15s

The clinical challenge

Amblyopia affects 5 million+ children in India. Early screening before age 7 is essential, but commercial equipment costs ₹2–3 lakhs.

STAGE 02 · 15-55s

Child-guided paths

The child chooses an engaging visual target (butterfly, fish, or dot) and follows 4 guided paths: horizontal, vertical, diagonal, and fixation jumps.

STAGE 03 · 55-70s

Objective report

100% on-device MediaPipe AI measures bilateral iris centroid symmetry and fixation stability. No video or photo is ever sent to the cloud.
