CLINICAL CRISIS · SOCIETAL IMPACT SLIDE 02 / 08

The Problem: Childhood Vision Loss

Over 5–10 million children in India suffer from strabismus (misaligned eyes) or amblyopia (lazy eye). 90% of cases can be treated successfully if caught before age 7–8. But traditional clinical equipment costs ₹1–3 lakhs and is unavailable in rural schools and anganwadis, leaving millions of children unscreened.

THE BREAKTHROUGH SOLUTION SLIDE 03 / 08

The Solution: NETRA-SCREEN

A smartphone-based, child-friendly pre-screening tool. Children simply follow an engaging animated butterfly or fish on screen, while the front camera and local AI analyze bilateral iris symmetry in 30 seconds—flagging children who need an ophthalmologist visit.

Traditional Clinical Barriers

• Children get frightened or bored by rigid medical chin-rests and dark examination rooms.
• Rural PHCs, Anganwadis and schools lack trained pediatric optometrists.
• Symptoms like mild lazy eye are invisible to parents until school failure occurs.

The NETRA-SCREEN Advantage

• Gamified: Children follow playful butterflies and swimming fish naturally.
• Edge AI: Calculates sub-millimeter iris centroid coordinates at 30+ FPS.
• Accessible: Any teacher, health worker, or parent can administer a test in 30 seconds.

SELF-EXPLAINING PIPELINE SLIDE 04 / 08

How It Works: 4-Step Self-Explaining Workflow

A transparent, deterministic pipeline combining pediatric psychology, computer vision, and clinical risk stratification.

STEP 01
🦋

1. Child Plays Target Game

Child follows a playful butterfly or fish moving along 4 calibrated paths: horizontal, vertical, diagonal, and jump-holds.

STEP 02
👁️

2. Real-Time Iris Centroid AI

Phone front camera tracks both iris centers at 30+ FPS, continuously verifying proper lighting, face angle, and steady posture.

STEP 03
📐

3. Binocular Symmetry Analysis

On-device algorithms calculate bilateral movement correlation, relative path differences, and fixation stability without invasive flashes.

STEP 04
📋

4. Instant Actionable Triage

Provides parents and health workers with a clear Green, Amber, or Red risk assessment advising when to see an eye doctor.

CLINICAL INTEGRITY & BOUNDARIES SLIDE 06 / 08

What NETRA-SCREEN Does vs. What It Does Not Do

Crucial ethical boundaries that protect patient safety and conform to pediatric medical device regulations.

What It DOES Do (Pre-Screening Tool)

  • ✓ Rapid, stress-free pre-screening on standard smartphones in schools and homes
  • ✓ Flags bilateral eye movement asymmetry and fixation instability for early doctor referral
  • ✓ 100% offline and private—no camera frames or child videos are ever recorded or uploaded
  • ✓ Generates transparent, repeatable mathematical metrics for research and health triage

What It Does NOT Do (Clinical Boundaries)

  • ✕ Does NOT provide a medical diagnosis of disease (it is a pre-screening triage guide)
  • ✕ Does NOT replace a comprehensive clinical exam by a certified ophthalmologist
  • ✕ Does NOT claim clinical accuracy until multi-center hospital clinical trials are complete
  • ✕ Does NOT collect personal patient data or require continuous internet connectivity

EVALUATOR DEFENSE & ROADMAP SLIDE 08 / 08

YIP Evaluator Defense & Clinical Validation Roadmap

Clear technical defenses addressing the top questions raised by clinical and engineering judges.

Q: How does NETRA-SCREEN handle natural child head movement?
A: Real-time 3D facial plane geometry isolates head tilt from ocular movement, pausing the session automatically if pose bounds are exceeded.
Q: Can this replace an ophthalmologist?
A: No. It is strictly a triage tool that identifies at-risk children early so parents can consult certified ophthalmologists in time.
Q: What are the next validation steps?
A: Controlled multi-center pediatric clinical trial comparing automated digital metrics against certified clinical cover tests.
