NETRA-SCREEN / 01 OF 08
THE RESEARCH OPPORTUNITY

A practical question.
A more accessible starting point.

Can an everyday smartphone make supervised eye-movement observation easier to run and document? NETRA brings the workflow together so that question can be studied.

First user

Ophthalmology researchers running supervised visual tasks. User interviews will establish the actual workflow and unmet need.

Potential buyer

A research institution or department. Purchasing authority, budget and willingness to pay remain to be established.

Longer-term possibility

School and community research, once the intended use, evidence and professional oversight support it.

First-user and buyer definitions are hypotheses. No signed pilot, paying customer or validated community deployment is claimed.

NETRA-SCREEN / 02 OF 08
THE PRODUCT EXPERIENCE

One guided session.
A structured observation.

Prepare

Choose a visual target, review guardian consent and check the camera setup.

Observe

Follow four paths: horizontal, vertical, diagonal and step-and-hold. Capture checks can pause the task.

Review

Review paired trajectories and experimental metrics. Export or print by explicit choice.

The camera-free walkthrough demonstrates target paths only. It does not generate participant measurements.

NETRA-SCREEN / 03 OF 08
ENGINEERING EVIDENCE

Built to demonstrate.
Designed to investigate.

Android + Web

Two working research implementations, with a bundled landmark model and a shared observation workflow.

On-device

Camera frames are processed locally. Camera images are not stored or uploaded. Report sharing is an explicit action.

Consent to report

Guidance, capture feedback, four paths and structured output. A dated engineering receipt documents automated software checks.

Engineering verification is documented in the partner pack. Real-phone performance, clinical accuracy and pediatric validity still require evidence.

THE TECHNICAL FOUNDATION

Privacy starts
at the point of capture.

CaptureExplicit permission, guided target paths and framing feedback.
LandmarksBundled MediaPipe Face Landmarker. The underlying model is third-party technology.
ObservationPaired trajectories, capture checks, experimental metrics and bilingual guidance.
PrivacyNo camera-image retention. Explicit report export. Offline web use follows a successful first download.

Our contribution is the research workflow around the model. Comparative clinical superiority and patent protection are not established.

NETRA-SCREEN / 05 OF 08
PROPOSED 90-DAY PLAN

The next 90 days.
Clear deliverables.

Benchmark

Days 1-30: define the phone matrix, measure repeat runs and framing failures, and interview potential research users. Deliver a baseline and needs summary.

Review

Days 31-60: work with a prospective clinician on the workflow and supervised usability protocol. Deliver a reviewed draft and a costed next work package.

Evaluate

Days 61-90: proceed when required review, consent and oversight are in place. Deliver usability findings and a go/revise decision, or document readiness gaps.

Proposed milestones, not commitments. Partner access, study requirements and budget determine timing. Clinical validation is a separate later study.

COMMERCIAL DISCOVERY

A focused first customer.
A business model to test.

Establish the need

Learn how research teams work today. Compare setup time, usable capture, supervision needs and report usefulness.

Understand the buyer

Explore institutional software with onboarding and support. Identify the budget owner and delivery costs before setting a price.

Earn the next step

Seek documented user problems and an agreed research evaluation. Demand, pricing, revenue and a funding amount remain unestablished.

Commercial discovery is the next workstream. Customer traction, market size and unit economics are not yet established.
