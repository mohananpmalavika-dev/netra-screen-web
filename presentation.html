NETRA-SCREEN / 01 OF 08
THE RESEARCH OPPORTUNITY

A practical question in pediatric eye care

Can a standard smartphone make guided eye-movement observation easier to study in community settings?

The intended setting

Schools and community programmes, with guardian supervision and professional research oversight.

The proposed approach

Use the front camera and engaging visual targets, without a dedicated capture accessory.

The question to validate

Whether the observations are repeatable, usable and clinically meaningful in the intended population.

Intended use is a research hypothesis. Community screening deployment has not been validated.

NETRA-SCREEN / 02 OF 08
THE PRODUCT EXPERIENCE

A guided session. A local observation report.

Prepare

Review consent, choose a butterfly, reef fish or focus dot, and check camera framing.

Observe

Follow horizontal, vertical, diagonal and step-and-hold target paths. Capture checks can pause the task.

Review

Inspect paired landmark paths and experimental movement metrics. Print or export by explicit choice.

The camera-free walkthrough demonstrates target paths only. It does not generate participant measurements.

NETRA-SCREEN / 03 OF 08
THREE PROOF POINTS

What the prototype demonstrates today

Two implementations

A native Android research app and a browser-based PWA, with a bundled landmark model.

Local processing

Live camera frames are processed on the device; no camera images are stored or uploaded.

A complete workflow

Consent, setup feedback, four guided tasks, trajectory plots and explicit report export.

Engineering capability is demonstrated. Clinical accuracy, pediatric validity and field performance require further evidence.

NETRA-SCREEN / 04 OF 08
THE TECHNICAL FOUNDATION

Designed around local processing

CaptureFront-camera frames with explicit permission; live and walkthrough sources remain separate.
LandmarksBundled MediaPipe Face Landmarker; paired iris centres relative to eye corners.
ObservationCapture checks, relative trajectories and experimental pursuit and fixation proxies.
PrivacyNo raw-image retention. Android supports encrypted opt-in summaries; web reports stay in session until exported.

First web load requires connectivity; offline use follows successful asset caching. Browser and device support must be verified.

NETRA-SCREEN / 05 OF 08
VALIDATION ROADMAP

Evidence before clinical deployment

Now

Rehearse the demo. Measure real-phone stability, battery use and frame acceptance. Review English and Malayalam instructions.

Next

Agree a supervised usability protocol with clinicians, including consent, participant suitability and stopping rules.

Then

Design an independent clinical comparison, define appropriate outcomes and assess repeatability before setting any referral thresholds.

Study sizes, timelines and budget will be agreed with research partners. No clinical classification is claimed.

NETRA-SCREEN / 06 OF 08
DEVELOPMENT & COMMERCIAL PATH

A partnership-led route to a viable product

Research partnerships

Explore supervised studies with ophthalmology teams and suitable institutions. No signed pilot or customer traction is claimed.

Cost hypothesis

The approach uses an existing smartphone. Device qualification, training, support and research costs still need to be measured.

Business model to test

Institutional software and support are possible routes after validation. Pricing, market sizing and unit economics are not established.

Current request: clinical collaboration, device-testing access and research mentorship. Funding follows a costed milestone plan.
