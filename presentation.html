THE FIRST CUSTOMER HYPOTHESIS

Start with ophthalmology research teams

Could guided capture and structured observation reports make supervised eye-movement research easier to run? This is the problem to test with users.

First user to explore

Ophthalmology researchers running supervised observation tasks. Interviews must establish the actual workflow and unmet need.

Potential buyer

A research institution or department, if it sees enough value in the workflow. Buyer interest and willingness to pay are untested.

Expansion to evaluate

School and community settings are a later research possibility, subject to appropriate evidence and professional oversight.

Customer and buyer definitions are hypotheses. No signed pilot, paying customer or validated community deployment is claimed.

NETRA-SCREEN / 02 OF 08
THE PRODUCT EXPERIENCE

A guided session. A local observation report.

Prepare

Review consent, choose a butterfly, reef fish or focus dot, and check camera framing.

Observe

Follow horizontal, vertical, diagonal and step-and-hold target paths. Capture checks can pause the task.

Review

Inspect paired landmark paths and experimental movement metrics. Print or export by explicit choice.

The camera-free walkthrough demonstrates target paths only. It does not generate participant measurements.

NETRA-SCREEN / 03 OF 08
ENGINEERING EVIDENCE

A demonstrable prototype. An auditable next step.

Two implementations

A native Android research app and a browser-based PWA, with a bundled landmark model.

A local workflow

Consent, capture checks, four guided paths and explicit report export. Camera images are not stored or uploaded.

Software verification

Automated checks cover core metrics, camera failures, privacy and responsive controls. The partner pack includes a dated evidence card.

Desktop browser tests establish software behavior. Real-phone benchmarks, clinical accuracy and pediatric validity remain unestablished.

THE PRODUCT CONTRIBUTION

A research workflow around an existing landmark model

CaptureGuided target paths, framing feedback and explicit camera permission.
LandmarksMediaPipe Face Landmarker is the underlying third-party model; model training is not claimed as our invention.
ObservationOur integration: paired trajectories, capture checks, experimental metrics and bilingual guidance.
PrivacyNo raw-image retention. Android supports encrypted opt-in summaries; web reports stay in session until exported.

Differentiation to test: workflow usability and device performance. Comparative clinical superiority and patent protection are not established.

NETRA-SCREEN / 05 OF 08
PROPOSED 90-DAY PLAN

Turn the next support into measurable deliverables

Days 1-30

Engineering lead + mentor: define a device matrix, record repeat runs and framing failures, and interview potential research users. Deliver a baseline and interview summary.

Days 31-60

Mentor + prospective clinician: review the workflow and prepare a supervised usability protocol. Deliver a reviewed draft and a costed work plan, subject to partner availability.

Days 61-90

Team + agreed study partner: run usability work only when the required review, consent and oversight are in place. Deliver findings and a go/revise decision. Otherwise document readiness gaps.

Proposed milestones, not commitments. Partner access, study requirements and budget determine timing. Clinical validation is a separate later study.

NETRA-SCREEN / 06 OF 08
COMMERCIAL DISCOVERY

Test the buyer and value before setting a price

Learn the workflow

Ask research teams how they run and document observations today. Compare setup time, supervision, usable capture and report usefulness.

Model to test

Institutional software plus onboarding and support is a possible route. Identify the budget owner and measure support costs before proposing a price.

What remains open

Customer demand, pricing, market size, unit economics and recurring revenue are unverified. No funding amount or valuation has been decided.

First commercial evidence: documented user problems, buyer conversations and an agreed next research step.
