Built as Android and web research prototypes. Our first customer hypothesis is ophthalmology research teams running supervised observation tasks.

PROPOSED MILESTONES

What the next support should produce.

Proposed 90-day work plan from an agreed start date. Timings depend on partner access, review requirements and resources.

DAYS 1-30

Baseline & discovery

Owner: team engineering lead + mentor; individual assignments to be agreed.

Define the phone matrix. Measure repeat runs, framing failures and session duration. Interview potential research users.

Deliverable: a performance baseline, known failure modes and a user-needs summary.

DAYS 31-60

Review & cost

Owner: mentor + prospective clinical collaborator.

Review the workflow, prepare a supervised usability protocol and obtain estimates for the next work package.

Deliverable: a clinician-reviewed draft and an itemised budget, subject to collaborator availability.

DAYS 61-90

Evaluate & decide

Owner: team + agreed study partner.

Proceed with usability work only when required review, consent and oversight are in place. Otherwise record the remaining readiness gaps.

Deliverable: findings and a go/revise decision, or a documented readiness assessment.

This plan does not promise clinical validation in 90 days. A clinical comparison is a separate later study. No funding amount has been set.

COMMERCIAL DISCOVERY

A specific customer hypothesis to test.

First user: ophthalmology researchers running supervised eye-movement tasks.

Potential buyer: a research institution or department. Demand, purchasing authority and budget remain to be established.

Model to explore: institutional software with onboarding and support, after the intended use and value are established.

What makes the product worth investigating?

Our contribution is the guided workflow around a third-party MediaPipe landmark model: target paths, capture checks, paired trajectories, experimental metrics and bilingual guidance.

Compare setup time, usable capture, supervision needs and report usefulness with the intended user's current workflow.

Comparative superiority, patents, signed pilots, revenue, pricing and market size are not established.
